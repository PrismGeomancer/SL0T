# SL0T launch film: NOTE

`film/final.mp4` is 1920x1080, 30 fps, 20.00 s, stereo AAC. `film/index.html` is the editable source. `film/audio/make_audio.py` regenerates the sound.

## The one idea
**The reels spin for 24 hours.** One lever pull is the whole launch window. The card's countdown runs while the reels blur and the curve stalls at 71%. At 00:00 the reels slam to a stop on 7 / cherry / BAR, one bulb pops and the room sags. Then the same lever, pulled again with 2x locked, lands 7 7 7. The second pull answers the first.
Cover-the-logo test: no other launchpad has "the reels are the window" and "a miss buys a second pull at 2x".

## Beats and what each buys
| f | role | the viewer believes |
|---|---|---|
| 0-102 | hook | this is a slot machine for coin launches (knob macro, yank, whip back, reels blur) |
| 102-232 | proof | the reels ARE the 24 h window: clock runs down, curve stalls, chip flips to MISS |
| 232-272 | turn | the result is physical (reels land, bulb pops) |
| 272-322 | turn | it's a MISS, and the machine says so, then "Run it back." |
| 322-400 | proof | a miss unlocks one rematch at 2x (REMATCH READY, 0.1 to 0.2 SOL, cursor presses Lock) |
| 400-528 | payoff | the second pull lands 777 (same knob macro as the hook), coin fountain, HIT., Claim 0.333 SOL |
| 528-600 | cta | name, line, who it is for (marquee SL0T, "Every launch is a pull.", "A friends-only launchpad on Solana.") |

## What is real and what is rebuilt
- **From the repo:** the 3D machine is ported from `app/src/three/machine.ts` (cabinet, three reel cylinders and symbols, 56 bulbs, lever, marquee, the site's own reel ease). Tokens, fonts (Bungee, Bungee Shade, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono), card, chip, curve bar, pool bar and button CSS are copied from `global.css`. Copy comes from `SL0T-copy-deck.md`: "Every launch is a pull.", REMATCH READY, "Lock 0.2 SOL on HIT", "$DUCK graduated.", "Run it back".
- **Numbers:** 0.1 SOL fee, 24 h window, 0.6 / 0.4 pool, 0.333 SOL payout are the worked example in the copy deck (Ana, $DUCK). The $FROG card values (0.100 HIT, 0.350 MISS, 71%) come from the demo data, not live chain data.
- **Illustrative:** the two coin icons, the countdown running in about 3.5 s (24 h compressed), the cursor, the coin fountain, all sound. Nothing is generated by an image model.
- **Not captured:** the cards are rebuilt from the site's CSS, not a screen recording of the live site.

## Gates (numbers from the scripts, final.mp4)
- G0 PASS: duration 20.00 s = planned 20.00 s, audio present, audible 99 %.
- G2 PASS: no run of more than 3 flat frames.
- G3 PASS: no held end card. The picture is still moving on the last frame (bulbs chasing, camera drifting). look.py reports "final shot 0.47 s" only because it counts the word entrances as cuts. The real last camera cut is f528, so the last shot is 2.40 s = 12 % of the film (limit 25 %).
- G5 PASS (lint.mjs), with one warning: V4 flags `$FROG` as zinc-grey text. It is the site's own `--muted` token.
- G1 and G4 are by eye, no script number. G1: card name 17 px x 2.9 = 49 px (0.046 H), small mono lines 15 px x 2.9 = 43 px (0.040 H). That is borderline, and the rematch card chip text (13 px x 2.6 = 34 px) is under 0.04 H. G4: during the card beats the 3D machine is defocused (CSS blur 7-9 px, dimmed) behind a card that is about 66 % of the frame width, so the card is the one hero. In the hook and 777 beats the machine is the one hero.
- Motion: mean frame-to-frame luma change 6.6 (human median 6.8, IQR 4.4-9.8), inside the band.
- Audio: -14.5 LUFS integrated, LRA 2.5 LU, true peak -1.3 dBFS, 4 of 15 detected cuts land within 80 ms of a sound onset. The cuts that matter (3.40, 10.73, 13.33, 17.60) have hits. 7.73 and 9.07 are 230-260 ms off an onset, which will read as slightly late or early.
- `Motion.selfTest` was NOT run (the render script does not print its result). Every dynamic 3D property is set absolutely each frame, and a full sequential render matched the direct-seek stills by eye.

## Frame descriptions (look-final/sheet.jpg)
0.6 s a glossy red knob in macro on a dark cabinet, "LAUNCH A COIN." under it (hero: knob). 1.9 s the whole machine with reels in blur and the marquee half cropped (hero: machine). 3.1 s same, "HIT OR MISS." over the lowest bulbs. 4.4-6.9 s a $FROG card at about 66 % W over a defocused machine, curve and clock moving (hero: card). 8.1 s reels decelerating, window larger than the frame. 9.4 s close on 7 / cherry / BAR with red "MISS." below. 10.6 s same window, "RUN IT BACK." in gold. 11.9-13.1 s the $DUCK rematch card with the machine defocused on the right, amount counting to 0.200, cursor on the button. 14.4 s whip, reels blurred. 15.6 s 777 landed, window glowing. 16.9 s coins in the air, green "HIT.", "$DUCK graduated.". 18.1 s full machine on a pink floor glow, coins falling. 19.4 s the same with "EVERY LAUNCH IS A PULL."

## Would still improve
- The mirror floor is faint (the frame only sees about 1.7 units below the base). A taller end frame or a lower camera would show more reflection.
- The end machine is about 65 % of frame height and the first and last frames of the hook crop the marquee. A reviewer may want the marquee in the first wide shot.
- Procedural sound is a sketch: the pad and arps are thin next to a composed track. ElevenLabs or a real score would lift it. There is no voice-over.
- No URL or button at the end because the repo has no public domain configured. Add one if you want a CTA line.
- Render needs `node --experimental-websocket` on Node 20 (the skill's scripts use the global WebSocket).
